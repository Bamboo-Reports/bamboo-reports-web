# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

GTM, sales, marketing, research, and strategy leaders evaluating India’s GCC market. They need reliable market coverage, account intelligence, and evidence that helps them prioritize opportunities and act with confidence.

## Product Purpose

Bamboo Reports makes the India GCC opportunity accessible and actionable through analyst-verified data, market intelligence, reports, and GTM enablement. The marketing site should establish Bamboo Reports as a credible GCC authority and convert qualified visitors into product exploration, account creation, or demo conversations.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, clear, and commercially useful. The experience should feel research-led and current without becoming academic, corporate-generic, or visually noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page decoration, ornamental dashboards, glassmorphism, gradient text, inflated metrics without context, repetitive card grids, and illustration-led layouts that compete with the message. Do not imitate editorial magazines or technical terminal interfaces.

## Design Principles

- Lead with a precise market promise that a visitor can understand within seconds.
- Make evidence and coverage feel earned, verifiable, and useful.
- Use hierarchy and whitespace to create authority rather than decorative effects.
- Preserve the established Bamboo Reports navy, blue, orange, and DM Sans identity.
- Keep primary actions obvious while letting serious buyers explore supporting detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain readable contrast and line lengths, visible keyboard focus, 44px minimum interactive targets, responsive layouts without text overflow, semantic structure, and reduced-motion behavior for all non-essential animation.
